## The learning sequence

Part one reframes failures as learning data. Part two covers records and team reviews. Part three discusses prioritizing recurring issues and building an improvement loop.

## What I created

Prepared a teaching scenario and three sets of materials, including record fields, review questions and a process for taking improvements into the next action, illustrated through educational examples.

## The scope of content production

Creating the materials is distinct from recording or delivering a course. Teaching examples and changes in grades or time described in slides are not presented as verified personal outcomes.
