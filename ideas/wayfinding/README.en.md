## One set of guidance rules

Proposed consistent zone colors, pictograms, overview maps and detailed maps. A QR scan would establish the visitor’s starting point for web-based guidance.

## Considering different journeys

Included nighttime access, wheelchair routes and a process for updating changed map information. The work considers both the on-site system and how its information is maintained.

## Deliverable and status

Submitted an individual application and a four-page proposal. The December 2025 result notice confirmed the entry was not selected for an award. The deliverable is a service proposal preceding implementation.
