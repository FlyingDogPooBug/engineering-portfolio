## Preserving cooling while reviewing power use

The proposal distinguishes conditions suitable for an operating adjustment from those requiring maintenance first. It plans to compare power before and after action under similar production loads and ambient conditions.

## Defining the service’s role

The initial concept provides measurement, condition monitoring and inspection support. Operators would review permissible conditions before changes, while existing protective devices and emergency stops remain in place.

## Preparation versus execution

Team 0ERROR prepared an application, idea proposal and portal-entry text. The status record says it has not been submitted. Savings targets and development plans are not presented as measured savings, acquired customers or a finished product.
