## The idea

The Pre-Lab Self Check concept asks researchers to review relevant safety items before an experiment and record the check through a QR form. Recurring issues could then be reviewed by the responsible staff.

## What I prepared

Wrote example check items, the proposed input-record-notification flow and a phased plan for piloting and revising the policy.

## Deliverable and status

The available deliverables are an application and policy proposal. They do not establish institutional adoption, a deployed QR system or a measured reduction in accidents.
