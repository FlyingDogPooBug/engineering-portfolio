## The starting question

I wanted to extend my interest in automotive technology to industry-wide production, demand and investment. I compared automotive production, shipments, inventories and business indicators with manufacturing, semiconductor and investment statistics.

## What I produced

The report describes using Excel for growth rates, moving averages and indicator comparisons. A presentation communicates the work. My focus was on which indicators to consider together and how to explain them.

## The scope of the work

This is an experience in working with statistics and documenting an interpretation. Similar movements in time series are not presented as proof of causation or as a business-performance improvement.
