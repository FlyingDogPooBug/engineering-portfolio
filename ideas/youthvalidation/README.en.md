## Support centered on a first experiment

The process connects a test plan, facility booking, safety education, execution, reviews and a results record. It preserves raw-data identifiers, personal roles, findings and limitations.

## Writing the operating conditions

Team 0ERROR prepared portal-entry text and a ten-page supporting document covering recipients, institutional capacity, support limits, budget calculations, evaluation, withdrawals and settlement.

## Proposal stage

Institution counts, team capacity, budgets and completion targets are design assumptions. They are not evidence of an operating program, policy adoption or improved participant skills.
