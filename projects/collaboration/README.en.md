## Checking requirements and data suitability

Contacted KATECH about damper-test conditions and Excel data, supplying specifications and drawings. I reviewed whether reference material suited our vehicle and asked about testing the actual dampers. Separately, received NEOTECH’s completion notice and dynamometer report for the dampers. My contribution was technical coordination and review; the provider performed the test.

## Connecting fabrication with team operations

Proposed ordering by technical subteam and a next-team handover process to MakeIT, receiving agreement. Confirmed receipt after the bellcrank was fabricated and delivered in person. At season’s end, connected the successor with the quotation-request process and received the supplier’s acknowledgement.

## Following up with evidence of use

- Reported fabrication-site use of Servore welding protection and season outcomes; the sponsor acknowledged internal sharing of the report and encouraged the next team.
- Shared NOVAX glove-use and publicity materials with GB Industries, which confirmed receiving photos and videos and acknowledged the team’s use.
- Reported NAS use and season materials to Synology. Managed continuity of KISSsoft’s existing software support and introduced the next leader, receiving confirmation of the new contact’s registration.

## Taking feedback into the next draft

After the Synology equipment was installed, I coordinated feedback on its use-case content. Worked with teammates and the photography club to refine photos and copy around practical use, then received approval to proceed. Subsequently shared Instagram and Facebook post insights and responded to follow-up material requests. My role was clarifying requirements, coordinating revisions and following up within a collaborative production process.

## Passing the relationship to the next season

Introduced the next team leader and clarified follow-up contacts for partners. Coordinated renewal and contact handover for KISSsoft’s existing software support, and forwarded SOLIDWORKS Student Sponsorship software and installation guidance to teammates and successors.

## Handling practical communication in English

Contacted an overseas component supplier on behalf of the team to request a delivery-information correction during an actual order. Alongside technical coordination, I handled the English correspondence needed to move practical work forward.
